The Practice

An accountant you can knock on the door of

For over five decades, Hans has prepared taxes for Utah County families, freelancers, and small businesses from his home office in Orem — by design. No corporate switchboard, no rotating cast of preparers. You talk to Hans.

His focus has never changed: saving clients money legally, and keeping them in good standing with the IRS. Today he works alongside his stepson Adam Daley, who brings more than a decade of experience with everything from simple W-2s to complex S-Corp returns — continuity that means the practice's next fifty years are in good hands too.

Why It Matters for the Campaign

The auditor's office is an accounting job. Hire an accountant.

Utah County's auditor oversees the county's books, budgets, and financial reporting. Hans is a licensed CPA with 40+ years of tax and accounting practice — individual, business, and estate work. He has spent his career being personally accountable for the accuracy of financial statements, with his license and his name on the line. That's precisely the discipline the county's checkbook needs.
