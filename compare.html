The 2026 Choice

Who is your best County Auditor choice in 2026?

Two candidates. On one side, her own published words. On the other, his answer. You decide.

Gina Tanner

Candidate for Utah County Auditor

"Let me be clear, in this explanation I am not arguing for or against the tax increases – I am simply setting the record straight. The County portion of property taxes remained flat for two decades. That means any additional revenue was solely from growth. Inflation was not accounted for, so we were losing ground each year."

— Gina Tanner, her campaign site, auditorgina.com
The tax history, from her site

"In 2019 the Commission did a Truth in Taxation Hearing and raised the property tax rate by 67.4% which was repealed down by 25% in April 2021. In 2024 there was another increase proposed of 47.99%. Again, the Auditor's role is to provide accurate information for the Commission to use in order to make policy and budget decisions."

— Gina Tanner, her campaign site, auditorgina.com
