Why Hans

An auditor should answer to you — not to city hall.

The County Auditor is the taxpayer's accountant. It's the one office whose whole job is to make sure your money is counted honestly, spent lawfully, and reported openly. Hans has spent his career doing exactly that — as a CPA for thousands of Utah County families and businesses, and as a public servant who was never afraid to ask the uncomfortable question.

  • He will not look the other way when public money is misspent.
  • He will be transparent with the public — every report, in plain English.
  • He will treat every taxpayer, business, and department equally.
  • He will oppose waste, hidden debt, and budget shell games.
  • He will be the voice of the people inside county government.
